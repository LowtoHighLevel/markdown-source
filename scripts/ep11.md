## Multiplication Explanation
First, Let's take a look at multiplication.

Functionally it is repeated addition, and we could implement it that way using a loop,
but that can be very expensive. I would like to look at a different option.

Since we're using binary, we can do a little bit of a cheat to make this work.
To multiply, we have two different numbers: The multiplicand and the Multiplier.

First, we check the lowest bit of the multiplier. If it is a 1,
 we add it to our result. Next, we shift our multiplicand to the left,
 and our multiplier to the right.

We continue this loop until our multiplier is zero, and we have our result.
